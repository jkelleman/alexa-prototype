    How it works

    One engine, two surfaces

    This page and the Alexa skill call the same dialog engine (skill/lambda/dialog.js). The copy, recovery rules, and screen content you see here are what the skill would say. Tests check the design principles directly: one question per turn, rephrase on recovery, and a screen that never shows a choice the voice didn't say.

    Principles

    1. One question per turn.
    2. Only claim what is built or what the user said.
    3. Recover from silence and misrecognition without blame.
    4. The screen supports the voice and never carries information the voice lacks.

    Honest status

    The skill passes local tests but hasn't been deployed or tested in the Alexa simulator. The screen design is untested on a device. Names come from a short list in this demo; say “call me ___” for any name. Independent portfolio project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon.
